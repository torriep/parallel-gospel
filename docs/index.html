Features

Synchronized Parallel Reading

All four gospels aligned in chronological order so you can see how each evangelist tells the same story.

9 Bible Translations

Choose from Louis Segond, King James Version, Darby, and more. Switch instantly or compare two translations side by side.

Word-Level Highlighting

See which words are unique to each gospel's account of a passage, highlighted automatically.

Search & Bookmarks

Full-text search across all four gospels. Bookmark passages and pick up where you left off.

Designed for iPad

Large readable text, adjustable font size, dark mode, and a clean interface built for focused study.

Completely Offline

No account, no internet required. Your data stays on your device.
