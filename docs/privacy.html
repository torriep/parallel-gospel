Privacy Policy

Parallel Gospel

Last updated: April 13, 2026

Overview

Parallel Gospel is committed to protecting your privacy. This app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data whatsoever.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not require an account, login, or internet connection to function.

Local Data

Your bookmarks, highlights, notes, and settings are stored exclusively on your device using local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Analytics & Tracking

We do not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising frameworks. There are no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Third-Party Services

The app does not connect to any third-party services. All Bible translation data is bundled within the app itself.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at pierre.roberge@gmail.com.
