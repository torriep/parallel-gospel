Frequently Asked Questions

Which Bible translations are included?

The app includes 9 public-domain translations in French and English, including Louis Segond (LSG), King James Version (KJV), Darby, and others.

Does the app require an internet connection?

No. All translations and data are included in the app. Everything works offline.

How are the gospels ordered?

Verses are arranged in chronological order across all four gospels, with 2,754 synchronized rows so you can compare parallel passages.

Can I use this on iPhone?

Parallel Gospel is designed for iPad to take advantage of the larger screen for side-by-side reading. iPhone support may come in a future update.

Is my data private?

Yes. All your bookmarks, highlights, and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not collect any personal data. See our Privacy Policy.

The app is crashing or not loading.

Try force-closing and reopening the app. If the problem persists, make sure you're running the latest version from the App Store, and contact us at the email above.
